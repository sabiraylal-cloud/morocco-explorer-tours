Desert

Mhamid & the Draa Valley

Palm-lined valleys and desert hospitality at the end of the southern road.

The journey to Mhamid follows the Draa Valley into southern desert country. A stay near Mhamid and an expedition to Erg Chegaga are different experiences, requiring different amounts of time.

Add to your journey ↗

TIME WELL SPENT

A few ways to explore.

  • Break the journey with stops in the Draa Valley
  • Share tea and settle into a desert overnight
  • Add days for a separately arranged Erg Chegaga excursion

PLAN WITH PERSPECTIVE

Before you go

Marrakech to Mhamid is a long journey. Two days means substantial driving; additional nights allow more stops and time in the desert.
