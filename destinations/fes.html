Imperial cities

Fes

Craft workshops, tiled gateways and the everyday life of a historic medina.

Fes rewards a full day of exploration. Its lanes connect working neighbourhoods, markets and architecture, so a visit is richer when there is time to stop and listen.

Add to your journey ↗

TIME WELL SPENT

A few ways to explore.

  • Begin your medina walk at Bab Bou Jeloud
  • Discover local craft traditions with a city guide
  • Take time for a lunch break between visits

PLAN WITH PERSPECTIVE

Before you go

Ask for a guided walking day rather than squeezing the city into a transfer afternoon. Comfortable shoes make the uneven streets easier.
