YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, in plain language.

How this website handles your trip-planning details.

The trip planner

The planner prepares a brief in your browser. This website does not submit the form to a server, store it in a database or save it in browser storage. Copying places the brief on your clipboard; downloading saves a text file on your device. Reloading the page clears the entered details.

Sharing your brief

If an email or WhatsApp option is available, using it opens that service with a draft message. Review the message before sending. The service you choose handles the information according to its own privacy terms.

Website requests

Images, styles and scripts are served with this website. No analytics, advertising trackers or third-party fonts are included in this version. The hosting provider may process standard connection information such as your IP address and request time to deliver and secure the site.

What to leave out

Do not enter passport numbers, payment details or sensitive personal information in the trip planner. A travel enquiry is not a confirmed booking.
