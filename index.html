Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your Morocco tours private?

Yes. Our tours are private and designed for you, your family, friends or private group.

Can I customize my itinerary?

Yes. We can adapt your itinerary according to your dates, interests, arrival city, departure city and travel style.

Do you provide airport pickup?

Yes. Airport pickup and drop-off can be arranged in Marrakech, Casablanca, Fes, Tangier and other cities.

Is camel trekking included in desert tours?

Camel trekking is normally included in our Sahara tours when specified in the itinerary.

What should I bring for the Sahara?

Comfortable clothing, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and a small overnight bag are recommended.

Can you arrange hotels and desert camps?

Yes. We can arrange carefully selected riads, hotels and desert camps according to the tour and your preferences.

Merzouga or Mhamid: which desert should I choose?

Merzouga is famous for its impressive Erg Chebbi dunes, while Mhamid offers a more remote and adventurous Sahara experience. We can help you choose the best option for your trip.
