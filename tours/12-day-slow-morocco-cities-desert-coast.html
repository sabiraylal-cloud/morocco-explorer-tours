YOUR ROUTE

Casablanca → Rabat → Chefchaouen → Fes → Merzouga → Skoura → Marrakech → Essaouira → Casablanca

A day-by-day starting point.

These itinerary ideas are flexible. Confirm the final schedule and overnight locations with us before booking.

DAY 01 Casablanca arrival

Begin with airport pickup, Casablanca's oceanfront and a soft landing night before longer touring starts.

DAY 02 Rabat to Chefchaouen

Visit Rabat's historic quarter, then continue into the Rif Mountains for Chefchaouen.

DAY 03 Chefchaouen at a slower pace

Keep time for the blue medina, viewpoints and a calmer mountain evening before heading inland.

DAY 04 Toward Fes

Travel to Fes with possible countryside stops, arriving with time to settle near the medina.

DAY 05 Fes in depth

Spend a guided day inside Fes, balancing monuments, craft workshops and unhurried meal breaks.

DAY 06 Middle Atlas approach

Head south through the Middle Atlas, breaking the road toward the Sahara with mountain and valley scenery.

DAY 07 Merzouga desert night

Continue through the Ziz Valley to Merzouga for your arranged camp or dune-side stay.

DAY 08 From dunes to palms

Leave the desert for the Todra or Dades area, then continue toward palm groves and kasbah country.

DAY 09 Ait Ben Haddou to Marrakech

Visit Ait Ben Haddou and cross the High Atlas into Marrakech.

DAY 10 Marrakech full day

Use a full day for guided medina exploration, gardens, hammam time or food experiences.

DAY 11 Essaouira coast

Travel to Essaouira for a slower Atlantic finish with ramparts, harbor scenes and sea air.

DAY 12 Casablanca return

Return to Casablanca with the day shaped around your flight time or final overnight.
