5-Day Atlas Valleys and Kasbah Roads Tour

A mountain-focused loop linking the High Atlas, Ounila Valley, Ait Ben Haddou and the palm groves around Skoura.

YOUR ROUTE

Marrakech → High Atlas → Telouet → Ounila Valley → Ait Ben Haddou → Skoura → Marrakech

A day-by-day starting point.

These itinerary ideas are flexible. Confirm the final schedule and overnight locations with us before booking.

DAY 01 Into the High Atlas

Leave Marrakech for mountain views, village stops and a slower crossing toward a valley guesthouse or lodge.

DAY 02 Telouet and the Ounila Valley

Follow the old caravan route through Telouet and the Ounila Valley, allowing time for viewpoints and local architecture.

DAY 03 Ait Ben Haddou and Skoura palms

Explore Ait Ben Haddou on foot, then continue to Skoura for palm groves and kasbah scenery.

DAY 04 Oasis rhythm

Spend a gentler day around Skoura and Ouarzazate, adding a short walk, a kasbah visit or downtime depending on your interests.

DAY 05 Back over the mountains

Return to Marrakech across the High Atlas with flexible stops for tea, views and arrival timing.

The pace of this trip

A scenic alternative to a desert dash. Expect winding roads and short walks rather than dune time, with more space for mountain and kasbah visits.
