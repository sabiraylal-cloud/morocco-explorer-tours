YOUR ROUTE

Casablanca → Rabat → Chefchaouen → Fes → Merzouga → Dades Valley → Skoura → Marrakech → Essaouira → Casablanca

A day-by-day starting point.

These itinerary ideas are flexible. Confirm the final schedule and overnight locations with us before booking.

DAY 01 Casablanca welcome

Arrive in Casablanca, settle in and begin with a relaxed introduction to Morocco's Atlantic gateway.

DAY 02 Rabat and the Rif road

Visit Rabat before heading north toward the Rif Mountains for a night en route to the blue city.

DAY 03 Chefchaouen day

Spend a full day in Chefchaouen, leaving space for blue lanes, viewpoints and slow cafe stops.

DAY 04 To Fes

Travel through northern countryside to Fes, arriving in time for a first medina impression.

DAY 05 Fes guided exploration

Explore Fes with a local guide, focusing on living craft traditions, architecture and the rhythm of the medina.

DAY 06 Fes at leisure

Keep a second day for deeper interests: cooking, ceramics, gardens, photography or simply a slower pace.

DAY 07 Middle Atlas and Ziz approach

Leave Fes through the Middle Atlas and continue toward the Ziz Valley, breaking the long road to the Sahara.

DAY 08 Merzouga desert arrival

Reach Merzouga and settle into your agreed desert camp, riad or dune-edge accommodation.

DAY 09 Sahara immersion

Use a full day for desert villages, music traditions, seasonal lake views, sunrise or quiet time near the dunes.

DAY 10 Gorges and Dades Valley

Leave the Sahara for Todra and Dades scenery, with stops shaped around light, walking comfort and road conditions.

DAY 11 Skoura and kasbah country

Continue through southern valleys to Skoura, where palm groves and kasbah architecture slow the pace.

DAY 12 Ait Ben Haddou and the Atlas crossing

Visit Ait Ben Haddou before crossing the High Atlas toward Marrakech.

DAY 13 Marrakech medina day

Spend a full day in Marrakech with guided souks, gardens or food experiences planned around your style.

DAY 14 Marrakech free day

Keep an unstructured day for rest, hammam time, shopping, a cooking class or a short countryside escape.

DAY 15 Atlantic finale

Travel to Essaouira for a coastal change of mood, ramparts, harbor views and a relaxed final night.

DAY 16 Return to Casablanca

Return to Casablanca for your flight or final hotel, with timing confirmed before booking.
