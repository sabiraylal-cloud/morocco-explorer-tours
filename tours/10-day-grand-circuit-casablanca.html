A fuller Casablanca loop through Chefchaouen, Fes, the Sahara, kasbah valleys, Marrakech and the Atlantic coast.

YOUR ROUTE

Casablanca → Chefchaouen → Fes → Merzouga → Dades Valley → Marrakech → Essaouira → Casablanca

A day-by-day starting point.

These itinerary ideas are flexible. Confirm the final schedule and overnight locations with us before booking.

DAY 01 Casablanca to the blue north

Start from Casablanca and travel toward Chefchaouen, arriving for an evening in the blue lanes.

DAY 02 Chefchaouen morning to Fes

Spend the morning in Chefchaouen before continuing to Fes for the next stage of the journey.

DAY 03 Fes guided day

Keep a full day for the medina, craft traditions and historic quarters of Fes with a local guide.

DAY 04 Middle Atlas to Midelt

Break the long desert approach with cedar forests, mountain scenery and an overnight around Midelt or the Ziz corridor.

DAY 05 Ziz Valley to Merzouga

Continue through the Ziz Valley to Merzouga, reaching the dunes for your arranged desert experience.

DAY 06 Desert morning to Dades

Wake early if you wish, then leave the Sahara for the Dades region, pausing for oasis and gorge scenery.

DAY 07 Kasbah road to Marrakech

Visit Ait Ben Haddou or nearby kasbah country before crossing the High Atlas to Marrakech.

DAY 08 Marrakech day

Use a full day for Marrakech's medina, gardens, food or craft stops depending on your interests.

DAY 09 Atlantic change of pace

Travel to Essaouira for sea air, ramparts and a relaxed final travel night outside the big cities.

DAY 10 Return to Casablanca

Return to Casablanca with transfer timing shaped around your flight or final hotel.
