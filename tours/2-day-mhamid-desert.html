2-Day Mhamid Desert Tour from Marrakech

A short southern escape across the Atlas and through the Draa Valley to the desert around Mhamid.

YOUR ROUTE

Marrakech → High Atlas → Draa Valley → Mhamid → Marrakech

A day-by-day starting point.

These itinerary ideas are flexible. Confirm the final schedule and overnight locations with us before booking.

DAY 01 Over the Atlas, into the Draa Valley

Depart Marrakech early, cross the High Atlas and continue south through Ouarzazate and the Draa Valley. Reach the Mhamid area for your agreed desert overnight. Stops and activities depend on travel time.

DAY 02 Desert morning, mountain homecoming

Enjoy the desert morning before beginning the long return to Marrakech. Break the journey along the way and arrive in the evening. Do not plan a departure flight that evening.

GOOD TO KNOW

The pace of this trip

Very long driving days and an early start in both directions. This short route is for the Mhamid area, not the distant Erg Chegaga dunes. Choose extra days for a more relaxed desert visit.
