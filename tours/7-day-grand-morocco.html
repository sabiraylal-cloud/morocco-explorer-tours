7-Day Grand Morocco Journey

Connect northern medinas, Sahara landscapes and Marrakech on a varied circuit, returning to Casablanca.

YOUR ROUTE

Casablanca → Chefchaouen → Fes → Merzouga → Ouarzazate → Marrakech → Casablanca

A day-by-day starting point.

These itinerary ideas are flexible. Confirm the final schedule and overnight locations with us before booking.

DAY 01 The Atlantic gateway and the blue north

Begin in Casablanca and head toward Chefchaouen. An early start gives you time to settle into the medina in the evening; late arrivals need a pre-tour night.

DAY 02 Blue lanes and a change of rhythm

Spend the morning exploring Chefchaouen before continuing to Fes for the night.

DAY 03 A day inside Fes

Allow a full day for Fes: its medina lanes, craft workshops and historic architecture. A local guide can be included in your personalised quote.

DAY 04 Mountain scenery and a desert night

Travel through the Middle Atlas and Ziz Valley toward Merzouga. Confirm your camp and desert activities during planning; arrival time determines the evening schedule.

DAY 05 Through the southern valleys

Continue west from the Sahara toward Ouarzazate, with stops around the Todra area as time allows. The landscape changes from open desert to valleys and earthen villages.

DAY 06 Kasbah paths and the High Atlas

Explore Ait Ben Haddou, then cross the High Atlas to Marrakech for your final overnight.

DAY 07 Marrakech moments and your onward journey

Arrange time in Marrakech around your transfer to Casablanca. Share your flight time before booking so the final day's schedule includes a comfortable transfer buffer.
