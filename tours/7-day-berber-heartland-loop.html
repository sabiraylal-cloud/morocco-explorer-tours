A Marrakech loop through mountain villages, kasbah valleys, Todra scenery, Merzouga dunes and the Draa Valley.

YOUR ROUTE

Marrakech → High Atlas → Ait Ben Haddou → Dades Valley → Merzouga → Draa Valley → Marrakech

A day-by-day starting point.

These itinerary ideas are flexible. Confirm the final schedule and overnight locations with us before booking.

DAY 01 Mountain villages and the old caravan road

Leave Marrakech for the High Atlas, pausing for views and village scenery before reaching the southern side of the mountains.

DAY 02 Kasbahs and the road of a thousand kasbahs

Explore Ait Ben Haddou, then continue through Ouarzazate toward Skoura or the Dades region.

DAY 03 Dades and Todra landscapes

Spend the day among valley roads, rock formations and gorge scenery, with the route shaped around walking comfort and timing.

DAY 04 To the dunes of Merzouga

Travel east through oasis towns to Merzouga for your planned desert arrival, camp transfer or dune-side accommodation.

DAY 05 A fuller Sahara day

Use a second desert day for sunrise, local villages, seasonal lake views or a quiet afternoon before sunset.

DAY 06 Draa Valley south road

Leave Merzouga and follow southern routes toward the Draa Valley, staying near palms and adobe villages.

DAY 07 Return to Marrakech

Cross back over the High Atlas to Marrakech, keeping the final evening free rather than planning a same-day flight.
