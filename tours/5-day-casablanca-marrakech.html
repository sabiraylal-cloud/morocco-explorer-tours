5-Day Casablanca to Marrakech Tour

A compact cross-country journey through Chefchaouen and Fes, with a night near the dunes of Merzouga.

YOUR ROUTE

Casablanca → Chefchaouen → Fes → Merzouga → Ouarzazate → Marrakech

A day-by-day starting point.

These itinerary ideas are flexible. Confirm the final schedule and overnight locations with us before booking.

DAY 01 Atlantic arrival, blue-city evening

Leave Casablanca early and travel north to Chefchaouen. Settle in for an evening walk through the blue medina. This is primarily a travel day; arrival flight times may require an extra night.

DAY 02 From the Rif to the medina

Enjoy a little more time in Chefchaouen before continuing to Fes. Explore nearby lanes after check-in; a full guided city visit requires an additional night.

DAY 03 Across the Middle Atlas to the dunes

Travel south through the Middle Atlas and Ziz Valley toward Merzouga. Plan for a full day on the road, with the desert arrival and any sunset activity dependent on timing.

DAY 04 Valleys, gorges and southern kasbahs

Leave the desert and continue toward the Todra area and Ouarzazate. Pause for scenery where the day's distance allows, then settle into your overnight stop.

DAY 05 An earthen village and an Atlas crossing

Visit Ait Ben Haddou before crossing the High Atlas to Marrakech. Finish at your accommodation; avoid a same-day early departure flight.

GOOD TO KNOW

The pace of this trip

Fast-paced, with several long driving days. Add nights in Fes and Marrakech for more time to explore; arrival in Casablanca the day before is recommended.
