8-Day Golden Light Photography Route

A slower visual route for photographers, linking early light in kasbahs, valleys, dunes and the medina of Fes.

YOUR ROUTE

Marrakech → Ounila Valley → Skoura → Dades Valley → Merzouga → Middle Atlas → Fes

A day-by-day starting point.

These itinerary ideas are flexible. Confirm the final schedule and overnight locations with us before booking.

DAY 01 Marrakech details and departure prep

Begin with a Marrakech walk focused on color, texture and practical route briefing before the road trip starts.

DAY 02 Ounila Valley frames

Cross the High Atlas and follow the Ounila Valley, timing stops for earthen architecture and layered mountain views.

DAY 03 Ait Ben Haddou to Skoura

Photograph Ait Ben Haddou in softer light, then continue to palm groves and kasbah angles around Skoura.

DAY 04 Dades formations

Spend the day around Dades scenery, choosing viewpoints and short walks that suit your gear and mobility.

DAY 05 To Merzouga dunes

Travel toward Merzouga and arrive with enough margin for your arranged desert transfer and evening light.

DAY 06 Sahara sunrise and desert textures

Use a full desert day for sunrise, changing dune patterns, village scenes and quiet time between shoots.

DAY 07 Ziz Valley to the Middle Atlas

Leave the desert through the Ziz Valley, breaking the drive with overlooks and a mountain overnight.

DAY 08 Fes arrival

Continue into Fes, where medina doors, tiles and evening lanes close the visual journey.
