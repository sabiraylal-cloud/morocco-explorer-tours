6-Day Marrakech to Fes Desert Crossing

Cross from Marrakech to Fes through Ait Ben Haddou, the Dades region and a full Sahara night near Merzouga.

YOUR ROUTE

Marrakech → Ait Ben Haddou → Dades Valley → Merzouga → Ziz Valley → Fes

A day-by-day starting point.

These itinerary ideas are flexible. Confirm the final schedule and overnight locations with us before booking.

DAY 01 Marrakech to the southern side of the Atlas

Cross the High Atlas and visit Ait Ben Haddou before continuing toward Ouarzazate or the Skoura area.

DAY 02 Valleys and rose country

Travel through the Valley of Roses and the Dades region, with time for viewpoints and a slower evening in the valley.

DAY 03 Gorges to the Sahara

Continue past Todra scenery and oasis towns toward Merzouga. Arrive for your agreed desert transfer and overnight.

DAY 04 Desert morning and local life

Wake for the desert light, then spend the day around the dunes, villages, music traditions or a relaxed camp-to-riad transfer.

DAY 05 Ziz Valley and Middle Atlas

Leave the Sahara through the Ziz Valley and climb toward the Middle Atlas for an overnight that breaks the journey to Fes.

DAY 06 Arrival in Fes

Continue to Fes with time to settle near the medina. Add a following day if you want a guided city visit.
