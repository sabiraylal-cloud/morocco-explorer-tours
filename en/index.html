Do I need to be fit to climb Toubkal?

You need good fitness and experience of walking uphill for several hours. In summer the ascent needs no climbing technique, but summit day is long. If you are unsure, the Imlil day trip is a good first taste.

When is the best season?

For treks, from late spring to autumn. In winter and early spring Toubkal is snow-covered and requires crampons and an ice axe. Day trips run all year round, weather permitting.

Where do we sleep on the treks?

Depending on the route, in mountain gîtes, at the Toubkal refuge or with village families. Comfort is simple, often in shared rooms or dormitories.

What should I bring?

Good walking boots, warm and windproof layers, a head torch, sun cream and a water bottle. Mules carry the luggage: keep the essentials in a daypack.

Do you pick us up in Marrakech?

Yes. Transport from Marrakech is included on every trip. Send us your accommodation address so we can confirm the pickup place and time.
