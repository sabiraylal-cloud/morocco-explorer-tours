OUR STORY

Our mountains, our villages,
our story.

We are guides from Marrakech and the High Atlas. We know these mountains from the inside: their trails, their valleys and the villages, the douars, that bring them to life.

WHO WE ARE

A small team
of local guides.

We are Mohamed and Hassan, a small team of local guides between Marrakech and the High Atlas. We did not discover these mountains, valleys and villages in a book: we know them from the inside.

We know the mule paths that link the douars, the families who open their doors to walkers, the muleteers who travel with us and the way the weather changes at altitude.

When you walk with us, you are not just following a route: you step into mountain life, at its pace and at yours.

WHY WE DO THIS

Sharing the real Atlas,
away from the crowds.

We love showing the Atlas the way we live it, away from the busiest routes. Each place has its own character:

  • The Zat valley: its douars and the families who welcome walkers for the night.
  • The Yagour plateau: its rock engravings and the silence of the high plateaus.
  • Imlil: the village at the foot of Toubkal, among walnut trees and mountain paths.
  • Toubkal: the highest summit in North Africa, reached at sunrise.
  • The Ourika valley: its river and its villages clinging to the slopes.

WHAT WE SHARE ON THE WAY

The rhythm
of the mountains.

We walk together on the mule paths, at a pace that leaves time to look around, talk and catch your breath. The mules carry the luggage; you only keep your day pack.

In the evening we stay with village families. We share mint tea, bread baked in the village and the meal, sitting together around the dish.

There is also the silence of the high plateaus, sunrise on the summit, and all the people we meet along the way: shepherds, muleteers, families from the douars.

ALONG THE WAY

Unforgettable moments

A few scenes from our trips in the High Atlas.

Tea on arrival

After the walk, the teapot arrives before the bags are even down: mint tea and bread still warm.

The silence of the Yagour

On the plateau, all you hear is the wind. We stop, listen and look at the engravings on the rock.

Sunrise on the summit

The people we meet

A shepherd sharing the path, a family inviting us in: in the mountains, the people you meet are part of the journey.

OUR VALUES

What matters to us.

01 / SMALL GROUPS

Groups on a human scale

Every trip is private, for your group only. We take the time to get to know everyone and adapt the pace.

02 / RESPECT

Respect for the villages

We enter the douars as guests: discreet, suitably dressed, and taking photos only with people’s consent.

03 / FAIRNESS

Fair work

The families who host you and the muleteers who travel with us are paid fairly for their work.

04 / SAFETY

Safety first

In the mountains, the weather and the group’s fitness decide. We adapt the route whenever safety requires it.
