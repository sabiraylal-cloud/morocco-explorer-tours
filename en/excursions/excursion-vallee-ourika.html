DAY TRIP

Day trip from Marrakech to the Ourika valley

The Ourika valley follows the river at the foot of the High Atlas, among earthen villages, orchards and small riverside restaurants. At the end of the road, the village of Setti Fatma is the starting point of a trail to a series of waterfalls.

Full day (about 8 h)DurationMarrakechStartAbout 65 km, 1 h 30 by roadDriveEasy to moderateLevel
Ask about this day trip ↗

YOUR ROUTE

Day programme

  1. 9:00Pickup from your accommodation in Marrakech.
  2. 10:00Stop at an argan cooperative and visit a Berber house.
  3. 11:30Arrive in Setti Fatma for a guided walk to the waterfalls (about 2 h return).
  4. 13:30Lunch by the river (optional).
  5. 15:30Drive back to Marrakech.
  6. 17:00Arrive at your accommodation.

HIGHLIGHTS

What you will experience

  • A women’s argan oil cooperative
  • A visit to a traditional Berber house
  • The village of Setti Fatma at the end of the valley
  • The walk to the Setti Fatma waterfalls

Included

  • Return transport from Marrakech with driver
  • Pickup and drop-off at your accommodation
  • Local guide for the waterfall walk

Not included

  • Lunch and drinks
  • Tips
  • Shopping and personal expenses
  • Anything not listed under “Included”

Age: 10 to 80 years. Minors (10–17) travel with a parent or legal guardian who is responsible for them.

Price on request.
The rate depends on the date and group size.

GOOD TO KNOW

The waterfall trail crosses rocks: good shoes are essential. After heavy rain, access may be limited. Times are approximate.
