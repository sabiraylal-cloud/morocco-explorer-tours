DAY TRIP

Day trip from Marrakech to the three Atlas valleys

A day to see several Atlas landscapes without a long walk: the orchards and cooperatives of the Ourika, the Moulay Brahim gorges and the market town of Asni, then the Imlil valley and its terraced villages, with stops at the viewpoints.

Full day (about 9 h)DurationMarrakechStartLoop from Marrakech with several stopsDriveEasyLevel

YOUR ROUTE

Day programme

  1. 8:30Pickup from your accommodation in Marrakech.
  2. 9:30Ourika valley: argan cooperative and Berber village.
  3. 11:30Drive to Asni through the Moulay Brahim gorges, with photo stops.
  4. 12:30Imlil valley: short walk and lunch (optional).
  5. 15:30Drive back via Asni.
  6. 17:30Arrive at your accommodation.

HIGHLIGHTS

What you will experience

  • The Ourika valley and an argan cooperative
  • The Moulay Brahim gorges and the town of Asni (Saturday market)
  • The Imlil valley and views of the Toubkal peaks
  • Lunch in a Berber village (optional)

Included

  • Return transport from Marrakech with driver
  • Pickup and drop-off at your accommodation
  • Guidance during the stops

Not included

  • Lunch and drinks
  • Tips
  • Shopping and personal expenses
  • Anything not listed under “Included”

Price on request.
The rate depends on the date and group size.

GOOD TO KNOW

Little walking: ideal if you want to see several Atlas landscapes in one day. The order of stops may change with the season and traffic. Times are approximate.
