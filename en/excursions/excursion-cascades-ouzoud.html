DAY TRIP

Day trip from Marrakech to the Ouzoud waterfalls

In the province of Azilal, the Ouzoud river drops from a cliff of about 110 metres into a green gorge. A path leads down to the foot of the falls, through olive trees and shaded terraces where Barbary macaques live.

Full day (about 10 hours)DurationMarrakechStartAbout 150 km, 2.5 to 3 hours by roadDriveEasyLevel
Ask about this day trip ↗

YOUR ROUTE

Day programme

  1. 8:00Pickup from your accommodation in Marrakech.
  2. 10:30Arrive in Ouzoud for a guided walk to the viewpoints, then down to the foot of the falls.
  3. 13:00Lunch facing the waterfalls (optional).
  4. 14:30Free time or boat ride (optional).
  5. 15:30Drive back to Marrakech.
  6. 18:00 – 18:30Arrive at your accommodation.

HIGHLIGHTS

What you will experience

  • The Ouzoud falls, about 110 metres high
  • The walk down to the foot of the falls along the olive-tree path
  • The Barbary macaques that live around the site
  • A boat ride at the foot of the falls (optional)

Included

  • Return transport from Marrakech with driver
  • Pickup and drop-off at your accommodation
  • Local guide on site

Not included

  • Lunch and drinks
  • Boat ride
  • Tips
  • Anything not listed under “Included”

Age: 10 to 80 years. Minors (10–17) travel with a parent or legal guardian who is responsible for them.

Price on request.
The rate depends on the date and group size.

GOOD TO KNOW

It is a long drive there and back in one day. The path to the foot of the falls includes steps. Please do not feed the monkeys. Times are approximate.
