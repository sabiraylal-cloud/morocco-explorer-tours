DAY TRIP

Day trip from Marrakech to Imlil

About 1 h 30 from Marrakech, Imlil is the starting point for hikes to Toubkal. This day trip lets you explore the valley on foot, among walnut trees, terraced fields and villages of earth and stone, with the peaks of the massif as a backdrop.

Full day (about 8 h 30)DurationMarrakechStartAbout 65 km, 1 h 30 by roadDriveEasy to moderateLevel
Ask about this day trip ↗

YOUR ROUTE

Day programme

  1. 8:30Pickup from your accommodation in Marrakech.
  2. 10:00Arrive in Imlil after passing through Asni.
  3. 10:00 – 13:30Guided walk to Aroumd through orchards and terraced fields, with a tea break.
  4. 13:30Lunch in Imlil (optional).
  5. 15:30Drive back to Marrakech.
  6. 17:00Arrive at your accommodation.

HIGHLIGHTS

What you will experience

  • The High Atlas road through the Moulay Brahim gorges and Asni
  • A guided walk to the village of Aroumd
  • Views of the peaks of Toubkal National Park
  • A tea break in a Berber village

Included

  • Return transport from Marrakech with driver
  • Pickup and drop-off at your accommodation
  • Local guide for the walk

Not included

  • Lunch and drinks
  • Tips
  • Personal expenses
  • Anything not listed under “Included”

Price on request.
The rate depends on the date and group size.

GOOD TO KNOW

A walk suited to most walkers. Bring good shoes and a warm layer: the mountains are cooler than Marrakech. Times are approximate.
