BOOKING TERMS

Terms and Conditions

The booking terms for private Morocco tours arranged with Morocco Explorer Tours.

These Terms and Conditions apply when you enquire about, book or travel on a private tour arranged by Morocco Explorer Tours. By confirming a booking, you accept these terms for yourself and for every participant included in your reservation.

1. Booking confirmation and payment

After your itinerary, travel dates, inclusions and final price are agreed, Morocco Explorer Tours will provide payment instructions. Unless different written terms are agreed for your booking, your reservation is confirmed when we receive a deposit of 20% to 30% of the total invoice amount.

The remaining balance is due according to the written proposal or invoice. For some tours, the balance may be payable in cash on the day of the tour. Your written proposal or invoice should be checked carefully before payment.

2. Booking changes and cancellations

If you need to change your booking, contact us as soon as possible. We will try to help, but changes depend on supplier availability, transport schedules and any costs already committed.

If you cancel your trip, cancellation fees are calculated from the date we receive written notice from you:

  • 30 days or more before the tour start date: the deposit is retained.
  • Less than 30 days before the tour start date: no refund is available and full payment may be required.

Different cancellation terms may apply if they are clearly stated in your written proposal, invoice or supplier conditions.

3. Events outside our control

We cannot accept liability for loss, damage, delay, additional expense or refund requests caused by events outside our reasonable control. These may include war or threat of war, civil unrest, airport closures, industrial action, terrorism or threat of terrorism, natural disasters, severe weather, road closures, public-health restrictions, government advice or any other circumstances that make travel unsafe or impracticable.

4. Special requirements

Please tell us before confirming your booking about dietary restrictions, medical needs, mobility needs, allergies or other requirements that may affect your trip. We will share relevant information with suppliers where needed to support your comfort and safety.

Requests such as preferred room type, hotel location or bed configuration should be made at the time of booking. We will try to arrange them, but they cannot be guaranteed unless confirmed in writing.

5. Transportation

Vehicles used for Morocco Explorer Tours services are arranged with licensed local transport providers appropriate for the number of passengers and the route. Seat availability, luggage space and vehicle type should be confirmed in your written proposal.

6. Travel insurance

All travellers are responsible for having suitable travel insurance. Your policy should cover personal belongings, money and passports, personal accident or injury, medical expenses, emergency medical treatment, repatriation and cancellation.

If your itinerary includes activities such as camel riding, horse riding, trekking, cycling, quad biking, mountain routes or travel above 2,000 metres, make sure your insurance covers those activities.

7. Liability and local suppliers

Morocco Explorer Tours arranges private travel services using local suppliers such as transport providers, accommodation providers, guides and activity operators. We are not responsible for injury, illness, death, loss, delay or damage caused by the acts, omissions or conditions of third-party suppliers where we have acted with reasonable care in arranging the service.

Safety standards, facilities and operating practices in Morocco may differ from those in your home country. Travellers are responsible for following local guidance, supplier instructions and reasonable safety advice.

8. Itinerary changes

Itineraries may change because of weather, road conditions, mechanical issues, supplier availability, driver or guide health, local events or other conditions beyond our control. Morocco Explorer Tours may adjust the route, timing or order of activities where needed for safety, comfort or practicality.

We recommend scheduling onward travel no earlier than the day after the end of a long private tour, as delays can occur on Moroccan roads and in remote areas.

9. Travel documents

You are responsible for checking passport, visa, entry, health and onward-ticket requirements for Morocco before travel. Morocco Explorer Tours is not liable for missed services, denied entry, repatriation costs or additional expenses caused by incomplete or invalid travel documents.

10. Activity equipment and safety

For activities such as cycling, trekking, horse riding, camel riding or quad biking, equipment availability and standards may vary by supplier and location. Bring your own specialist safety equipment where required by your insurance, comfort level or home-country standards.

11. Privacy and use of information

Personal information provided during enquiry or booking is used to arrange travel services and communicate with you about your trip. Relevant details may be shared with hotels, transport providers, guides and activity suppliers. Read our Privacy Policy for more information.

We may request permission to use photographs, feedback or case studies from your tour on our website or social media. If you do not want to be featured, tell us before or during your trip.

12. Complaints

If something goes wrong during your tour, tell your driver, guide, accommodation provider or Morocco Explorer Tours as soon as possible so we have a chance to resolve the issue while you are still travelling.

If the issue cannot be resolved locally, contact us with full details. We will review the matter and respond as fairly as possible based on the services booked, supplier information and circumstances.
