BOOKING TERMS

Terms and Conditions

The rules that apply to our treks and day trips in the High Atlas. Please read them before booking: they protect you as much as they commit us.

These terms and conditions apply to all treks and day trips organised by Morocco Explorer Tours, and to every related enquiry and booking.

By confirming a booking, you declare that you have read and accepted these terms, for yourself and for everyone booked with you. The person making the booking agrees to share them with every participant in their group.

1. Our trips

We organise multi-day treks and day trips in the High Atlas from Marrakech. The programme, level, stages, and what is and is not included are described on each trip’s page and confirmed in our written proposal. Where they differ, the written proposal you accepted prevails.

The itineraries on the website are model programmes: in the mountains they may be adapted for safety, weather or organisational reasons (see sections 6, 8 and 10).

2. Booking

You can contact us by email, WhatsApp or through the website’s “Plan your trip” tool. An enquiry is not a booking. We send you a written proposal setting out the programme, dates, price and services.

Once you accept the proposal, we send you an invoice with payment instructions. Your booking is confirmed when we receive the deposit; we then send you a written confirmation.

For each participant we may ask for: full name, age, nationality and passport number (for the registration required by some accommodation), an emergency contact, and any health information relevant to their safety (see section 7).

3. Prices, deposit and payment

Our prices are quoted on request, according to the date, the trip and the group size. The validity period of each quote is stated on the quote itself.

  • Deposit: 20% to 30% of the total invoice amount, paid to confirm the booking. The exact percentage is shown on the invoice.
  • Balance: it may be paid in cash on the day the trek or day trip starts.
  • Payment methods: bank card (Visa, Mastercard, Maestro), PayPal or Google Pay through secure online payment, and cash for the balance. Any bank or exchange fees are paid by the client.

If the balance is not paid on the day of departure, we may treat the booking as cancelled by the client and apply the charges in section 5.

4. What is and is not included

The services included and not included are listed under “Included” and “Not included” on each trip page, then confirmed in the written proposal. Anything not listed there is not included, in particular accommodation in Marrakech, insurance, drinks outside meals, equipment hire and tips, unless stated otherwise.

5. Cancellation or changes by the client

Any cancellation must be sent to us in writing (email or WhatsApp message) as soon as possible. Charges are calculated from the date we receive your written cancellation, and apply to treks and day trips alike:

  • 30 days or more before the start date: only the deposit is retained; the rest of what you have paid is refunded.
  • Less than 30 days before the start date, or no-show: no refund is possible and the full price is due.

A change of date requested by the client is possible subject to availability. If the change cannot be made, you may keep your original booking or cancel it under the terms above.

A day, stage or service not used because of you (late arrival, leaving the trip, early departure) is not refunded. We strongly recommend cancellation insurance.

6. Cancellation or changes by Morocco Explorer Tours

We may need to change or cancel a trip before departure if safety cannot be ensured (weather, snow, floods, closed roads or refuges, decisions by the authorities) or if the guide becomes unexpectedly unavailable.

If we cancel before departure, we offer another date or an equivalent trip; if you decline, we refund everything you have paid in full. No further compensation is due when the cancellation is related to safety, weather or force majeure.

If we have to make a significant change to an essential part of the trip before departure, we will tell you as soon as possible: you can accept the change or cancel free of charge.

7. Fitness, health and altitude

Our treks take place in the mountains, far from rescue services and medical facilities. By booking, each participant declares that they are in good health and have a level of fitness suited to the chosen trip as described on its page (walking time, ascent, altitude).

You must tell us before booking about any medical condition, chronic illness, recent injury, ongoing treatment, allergy, pregnancy or mobility difficulty. This information stays confidential and is shared only with the guide, for your safety. If in doubt, consult your doctor before travelling.

The Toubkal ascent reaches 4,167 m. Above 2,500 to 3,000 m, acute mountain sickness can affect anyone, however fit: headache, nausea, severe tiredness, poor sleep. Add cold, wind, sun, snow or ice depending on the season, and steep or unstable ground. Symptoms must be reported to the guide immediately; often the only safe treatment is to descend.

The guide may refuse to let a participant start or continue a trek if their condition puts themselves or the group at risk. An undeclared medical condition may lead to exclusion from the trip without refund.

8. The guide’s authority

During the trip, the guide is responsible for the group’s safety. Their decisions about the route, timing, pace and whether to continue must be followed.

They may at any time change, shorten, delay or stop an itinerary, give up a summit or pass, or send a participant back down (accompanied), in particular because of the weather, snow or ice, river levels, the state of the ground, the time of day, the group’s pace, or a participant’s health or tiredness.

These decisions, taken for everyone’s safety, give no right to a refund for the parts not completed. Any resulting extra costs (extra night or transport, evacuation) are paid by the participant or their insurer.

9. Insurance

Travel insurance is required for every trek participant and strongly recommended for day trips. It must cover at least:

  • medical and hospital costs abroad;
  • mountain search, rescue and evacuation, including by helicopter where available;
  • repatriation;
  • mountain trekking up to the trip’s altitude (4,200 m for Toubkal) and, in winter, walking on snow with crampons and an ice axe.

Cancellation and baggage cover is strongly recommended. We may ask for proof of insurance before departure. In the High Atlas, evacuation is often on foot, by mule or by vehicle to the nearest road, and air rescue is not guaranteed: rescue and evacuation costs remain the responsibility of the participant or their insurer.

10. Weather and force majeure

Mountain conditions change quickly. Itineraries may be adapted or cancelled because of weather, snow, floods, rockfall, closed roads, tracks or refuges, or decisions by the authorities.

We cannot be held responsible for a delay, change or cancellation caused by an external, unforeseeable and unavoidable event (force majeure): natural disaster, exceptional weather, earthquake, epidemic, unrest, strikes, administrative decisions. In such cases we offer an alternative where possible; any extra costs remain the client’s responsibility.

11. Inherent risks of mountain activities and liability

Hiking and trekking in the mountains involve risks that cannot be entirely removed: falls, slips, rockfall, unstable ground, river crossings, extreme cold and heat, altitude, contact with animals (mules, dogs), and distance from rescue and medical care. By booking, each participant acknowledges being informed of these risks and accepts them.

We organise our trips with care and work with guides and local providers chosen for their reliability. We are not liable for damage resulting from: failure to follow the guide’s instructions; undeclared health information; the participant’s own behaviour; a provider or third party unconnected with the organisation of the trip; or force majeure. We are not responsible for the loss or theft of, or damage to, personal belongings.

To the extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to the price paid for the trip, except in the case of gross negligence or wilful misconduct, and except for personal injury where the law does not allow liability to be limited.

12. Mules and luggage

On treks, luggage is carried by mules led by a muleteer. Each participant may bring one soft bag, within the weight limit given to them before departure; hard suitcases are not accepted. Above this limit, a supplement may be charged or the excess left in Marrakech.

Mules do not always take the same path as the group and may arrive after you: keep water, warm and waterproof clothing, sun protection, medication, documents and valuables in your daypack. We do not overload the animals, and we accept no responsibility for fragile items packed in the luggage.

13. Mountain accommodation

On treks you sleep in mountain gîtes, refuges, family homes or camps, depending on the programme. Comfort is simple: shared rooms or dormitories, mattresses on the floor, shared bathrooms, sometimes limited hot water and electricity, rarely heating. Refuges are shared with other hikers and places depend on availability.

Homestay nights are spent in family houses: please respect your hosts’ privacy and habits. We tell you when you book whether a sleeping bag is provided or whether you need to bring one.

14. Age of participants and minors

Our treks and day trips are open to participants aged 10 to 80.

Minors aged 10 to 17 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, who remains responsible for them throughout the trip. The guide may refuse to let a minor take part in a stage they consider unsuitable.

15. Behaviour, local customs and the environment

Each participant agrees to respect the other group members, the guide, the muleteer, the families hosting us and the village residents. Modest clothing is expected in villages; always ask before photographing someone; any alcohol must be consumed discreetly and must not compromise safety.

We travel through fragile places: stay on the trails, carry out your rubbish, do not damage plants and do not touch the rock engravings, which are protected heritage. Dangerous, disrespectful or illegal behaviour may lead to exclusion from the trip without refund.

16. Photos and videos

During our trips, the guide or our team may take photos and videos in which participants may appear.

We only use a photo or video in which you are recognisable on our website or social media with your consent. Consent is optional: you can give it by ticking the box on the enquiry form, or by confirming it to us in writing. Saying no has no effect on your booking.

You can withdraw your consent at any time and ask us to remove a photo or video by emailing contact@moroccoexplorertours.com or by WhatsApp. We remove it from our website and accounts as soon as possible; we cannot control copies already shared by third parties.

We never use a photo of a child without the explicit written consent of their parent or legal guardian.

17. Complaints

If something goes wrong during the trip, tell the guide straight away so we can put it right on the spot. Any complaint must then be sent to us in writing, with supporting details, within 15 days of the end of the trip, to contact@moroccoexplorertours.com.

18. Personal data

The information you give us is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

19. Applicable law and disputes

These terms are governed by Moroccan law. In the event of a dispute, we will first seek an amicable solution. Failing that, the competent courts of Marrakech will have sole jurisdiction, without prejudice to mandatory consumer-protection rules in the client’s country of residence.
