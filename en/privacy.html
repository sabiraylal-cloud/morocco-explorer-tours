YOUR DATA

Privacy Policy

What information we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how to exercise your rights.

The data controller is Morocco Explorer Tours. For any question or request about your data, email us at contact@moroccoexplorertours.com or message us on WhatsApp.

We process your data in accordance with Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, under the supervision of the National Commission for the Control of Personal Data Protection (CNDP). If you live in the European Union or European Economic Area, we also apply the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. The data we collect

We only collect the data needed for your request and your trip:

  • When you contact us (email, WhatsApp, phone, social media): your name, contact details and the content of your messages.
  • When you use the “Plan your trip” tool: the brief is created in your browser and nothing is sent automatically. We only receive this information if you choose to send it to us by email or WhatsApp (dates, number of walkers, level, wishes, your choice about photos).
  • When you book: participants’ names, age or date of birth, nationality and passport number where accommodation registration requires it, an emergency contact, dietary preferences, insurance details and payment records.
  • Health information: only what you give us for your safety (section 7 of the terms and conditions), with your explicit consent.
  • Photos and videos taken during the trip, used publicly only with your consent (see section 6).
  • Technical browsing data kept by our hosting provider (see section 8).

2. Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiries and prepare a proposal (pre-contractual steps at your request).
  • To organise and run your trip: guide, muleteer, accommodation, transport (performance of the contract).
  • To keep you safe in the mountains and respond to emergencies (vital interests, and consent for health data).
  • To meet our legal obligations: accounting, invoicing, accommodation registration.
  • To publish photos or videos in which you appear (consent, which you can withdraw at any time).

We do not sell or rent your data and do not use it for targeted advertising. We do not send you marketing without your consent.

3. Who can access it

Only the people who need your data for your trip receive it, and only what is necessary: our guide and muleteer, the gîtes, refuges and families hosting you, our transport providers and, in an emergency, rescue services, doctors and your insurer. We may also be required to share certain information with the authorities where the law requires it.

We use communication and hosting tools provided by third-party companies: WhatsApp messaging (Meta), our email service and our web host (Hostinger). Some of these providers may process data outside Morocco. We handle these transfers in accordance with Law 09-08 and, where applicable, the GDPR, including the formalities required by the CNDP.

4. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries without a booking: as long as needed to answer you, then deleted if no booking follows.
  • Booking and invoicing data: for the duration of the trip, then for the period required by our accounting and tax obligations.
  • Passport numbers and health information: deleted after the end of the trip, unless the law requires otherwise.
  • Published photos and videos: until you withdraw your consent.
  • Website technical logs: according to the retention period applied by our host.

5. Your rights

Under Law 09-08, you have the right to access and correct your data, to object to its processing on legitimate grounds, and to ask for its deletion when it is no longer needed. If the GDPR applies to you, you also have the right to restrict processing and to data portability.

You can withdraw any consent at any time, including for the use of your photos, without affecting processing already carried out.

To exercise your rights, email contact@moroccoexplorertours.com. We may ask you to prove your identity, and we reply within one month. You can also complain to the CNDP (www.cndp.ma) or, if you live in the European Union, to your country’s data protection authority.

6. Photos and videos

We may take photos and videos during our trips. We only use an image in which you are recognisable on our website and social media with your consent, given by ticking the box on the enquiry form or in writing. Consent is optional.

You can withdraw it at any time and ask us to remove a photo or video by emailing contact@moroccoexplorertours.com or by WhatsApp: we remove it from our website and accounts as soon as possible. We never use a photo of a child without the explicit written consent of their parent or legal guardian.

7. Security

We protect your data with appropriate measures: access limited to the people who need it, password-protected accounts, and deletion of sensitive information as soon as it is no longer needed. As no system is perfectly secure, please never send us a payment card number by message.

8. Cookies and third-party services on this website

This website uses no cookies, no audience-measurement tools, no advertising trackers and no fonts or scripts loaded from external services. The “Plan your trip” tool runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing.

The website is hosted by Hostinger, whose servers and content delivery network record technical data (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type) needed to display and secure the site.

The website contains plain links to third-party services: WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TourRadar and a few information websites. These services are not loaded until you click the link; once on their site, their own privacy policies apply. The administration area used only by our team runs Decap CMS (via jsDelivr) and GitHub; visitors do not use it.

If we ever add an audience-measurement tool or non-essential cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

9. Minors

Data about a minor is given to us by their parent or legal guardian, who also exercises the minor’s rights on their behalf.

10. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services or the law. The date of the last update is shown at the bottom of this page.
