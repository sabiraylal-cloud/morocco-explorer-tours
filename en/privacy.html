Privacy Policy

How Morocco Explorer Tours collects, uses and protects information shared through this website and during trip planning.

Morocco Explorer Tours respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when you browse this website, contact us, request a private Morocco tour or continue with a booking.

1. Why we collect personal information

We collect personal information so we can answer enquiries, prepare travel proposals, arrange private tours, manage bookings, provide customer support and meet legal or operational requirements related to travel services.

Depending on the service requested, Morocco Explorer Tours may act as the data controller for retail travel services, enquiries and marketing communications.

2. Personal information we may collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with us. It may include your name, email address, phone number, travel dates, arrival and departure details, group size, accommodation preferences, tour interests and any notes you choose to share.

When needed to arrange a confirmed trip, we may ask for passport or travel-document details, dietary requirements, medical or accessibility information, emergency contact details and payment-related information. Sensitive information is requested only when relevant to your travel arrangements and with your consent.

3. How we collect information

We collect information directly from you when you contact us by email, WhatsApp, phone, social media or an online form; when you request a quote; when you confirm a tour; or when someone travelling with you provides details on your behalf.

The trip planner on this website prepares a brief in your browser. It does not automatically submit the form to a server or store your answers in browser storage. If you choose to copy, download, email or send the brief by WhatsApp, the selected service handles that information under its own privacy terms.

4. How we use your information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, design itineraries, confirm availability, process bookings, coordinate hotels, transport, guides and activities, provide travel updates, improve our services and comply with applicable laws.

We may also use your contact details to follow up on a requested proposal or booking. We only send marketing messages where permitted by law or where you have chosen to receive them.

5. Sharing information with suppliers

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share only the information needed to provide your travel services with trusted suppliers such as accommodation providers, transport operators, local guides, activity providers, payment processors and technology providers.

Suppliers receive only the details required for their part of the service, including relevant dietary, medical or accessibility information when necessary for your safety and comfort.

6. International transfers

Travel planning may require information to be shared with suppliers or service providers outside your home country. We take reasonable steps to ensure that personal information is handled appropriately and only for the purpose of arranging or supporting your requested travel services.

7. Cookies and website data

This website uses local files such as images, styles and scripts to provide the browsing experience. The current version does not include advertising trackers, third-party fonts or analytics scripts. Standard hosting logs may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested page and request time to deliver and secure the site.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, update, restrict or delete personal information we hold about you. You may also object to certain processing or request a copy of your information in a portable format.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Security and retention

We use reasonable physical, electronic and procedural safeguards to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose collected, including booking administration, legal, accounting, dispute-resolution and service-improvement requirements.

10. Third-party links and services

Our website may link to third-party services such as WhatsApp, email apps, social media platforms or review and booking platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and you should review those policies before sharing information with them.

11. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date. Continuing to use the website or our services after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
