4-DAY TREK

4-day trek: Toubkal circuit via the Azzaden valley

For those who want more than the classic route. You approach Toubkal from the west, through the Azzaden valley with its stone villages and walnut trees, then cross a high pass before climbing the summit. A longer, more demanding route, but far more varied.

4 days / 3 nightsDurationMarrakechStartChallenging to demandingLevel4,167 m (Toubkal)Highlight
Ask about this trek ↗

YOUR ROUTE

Marrakech → Imlil → Tizi n’Mzik → Tizi Oussem → Azib Tamsoult → Tizi n’Aguelzim → Toubkal refuge → Toubkal summit → Aroumd → Imlil → Marrakech

Day by day

DAY 01 Marrakech – Imlil – Tizi n’Mzik – Tizi Oussem

Drive to Imlil, about 1 h 30. Climb through the villages to the Tizi n’Mzik pass, then descend into the Azzaden valley to the village of Tizi Oussem.

Walking
5–6 h
Altitude
Imlil 1,740 m → Tizi n’Mzik 2,489 m → Tizi Oussem approx. 1,850 m
Overnight
Gîte in Tizi Oussem
Meals
Lunch, dinner
DAY 02 Tizi Oussem – Azib Tamsoult – Tizi n’Aguelzim – Toubkal refuge

Walk up the Azzaden valley to the Azib Tamsoult shepherds’ huts and the Irhoulidene waterfall, then a long climb to the Tizi n’Aguelzim pass. On the far side, descend to the Toubkal refuge. This is the hardest day of the trek. Depending on the pass, the mules reach the refuge via the Imlil valley: keep the essentials in your daypack.

Walking
8–9 h
Altitude
Tizi Oussem approx. 1,850 m → Tizi n’Aguelzim approx. 3,560 m → refuge 3,207 m
Overnight
Toubkal refuge (dormitory)
Meals
Breakfast, lunch, dinner
DAY 03 Toubkal summit (4,167 m) – Aroumd

Start before dawn for the summit. Back to the refuge, then descend via Sidi Chamharouch to the village of Aroumd.

Walking
8–9 h
Altitude
Refuge 3,207 m → summit 4,167 m → Aroumd approx. 1,900 m
Overnight
Gîte in Aroumd
Meals
Breakfast, lunch, dinner
DAY 04 Aroumd – Imlil – Marrakech

A short walk down to Imlil, then the drive back to Marrakech.

Walking
approx. 1 h
Altitude
Aroumd approx. 1,900 m → Imlil 1,740 m
Overnight
End of trek (Marrakech not included)
Meals
Breakfast, lunch

HIGHLIGHTS

What you will experience

  • The Tizi n’Mzik pass and its view over the Azzaden valley
  • The village of Tizi Oussem and its terraced fields
  • The Irhoulidene waterfall near the Azib Tamsoult shepherds’ huts
  • Crossing the Tizi n’Aguelzim pass, around 3,560 m
  • The summit of Toubkal (4,167 m)

Included

  • Transport Marrakech – Imlil – Marrakech
  • Mountain guide
  • Mules and muleteer for luggage
  • All accommodation during the trek (gîtes and refuge)
  • All meals during the trek

Not included

  • Accommodation in Marrakech
  • Drinks outside meals
  • Travel insurance
  • Equipment hire (crampons, ice axe, sleeping bag)
  • Tips
  • Anything not listed under “Included”

Age: 10 to 80 years. Minors (10–17) travel with a parent or legal guardian who is responsible for them.

Price on request.
The rate depends on the date and group size.

GOOD TO KNOW

For very fit hikers who are comfortable on steep trails and scree. The Tizi n’Aguelzim pass can hold snow into spring: the route is confirmed according to the season.
