4-DAY TREK

4-day trek: Toubkal ascent by the classic route

The most direct route to Toubkal. You climb gradually from the Imlil valley, sleep at the refuge below the summit and set off before dawn to reach the roof of North Africa, before heading back down to the villages.

4 days / 3 nightsDurationMarrakechStartChallengingLevel4,167 m (Toubkal)Highlight
Ask about this trek ↗

YOUR ROUTE

Marrakech → Imlil → Aroumd → Sidi Chamharouch → Toubkal refuge → Toubkal summit → Imlil → Marrakech

Day by day

DAY 01 Marrakech – Imlil – Aroumd

Leave Marrakech in the morning, about 1 h 30 by road via Asni. In Imlil the mules are loaded and we walk up through walnut trees and orchards to the village of Aroumd. A quiet afternoon to acclimatise around the village.

Walking
2–3 h
Altitude
Imlil 1,740 m → Aroumd approx. 1,900 m
Overnight
Gîte (mountain guesthouse) in Aroumd
Meals
Lunch, dinner
DAY 02 Aroumd – Sidi Chamharouch – Toubkal refuge

Cross the Aroumd flood plain, then follow the valley up to the shrine of Sidi Chamharouch, a pilgrimage site beside the stream. The trail then steepens all the way to the refuge at the foot of Toubkal’s slopes.

Walking
5–6 h
Altitude
Sidi Chamharouch 2,310 m → refuge 3,207 m
Overnight
Toubkal refuge (dormitory)
Meals
Breakfast, lunch, dinner
DAY 03 Toubkal summit (4,167 m) – Imlil

Start before dawn for the ascent over scree and ridges. From the summit the view opens over the High Atlas ridges. Back to the refuge, then a long descent via Sidi Chamharouch to Imlil.

Walking
8–9 h
Altitude
Refuge 3,207 m → summit 4,167 m → Imlil 1,740 m
Overnight
Gîte in Imlil
Meals
Breakfast, lunch, dinner
DAY 04 Imlil – Marrakech

A relaxed morning in Imlil, then the drive back to Marrakech, arriving in the early afternoon.

Overnight
End of trek (Marrakech not included)
Meals
Breakfast, lunch

HIGHLIGHTS

What you will experience

  • The summit of Toubkal (4,167 m), the highest point in North Africa
  • The village of Aroumd and its terraced fields
  • The shrine of Sidi Chamharouch beside the mountain stream
  • A night in a refuge at the heart of Toubkal National Park
  • Daybreak over the High Atlas ridges

Included

  • Transport Marrakech – Imlil – Marrakech
  • Mountain guide
  • Mules and muleteer for luggage
  • All accommodation during the trek (gîtes and refuge)
  • All meals during the trek

Not included

  • Accommodation in Marrakech
  • Drinks outside meals
  • Travel insurance
  • Equipment hire (crampons, ice axe, sleeping bag)
  • Tips
  • Anything not listed under “Included”

Age: 10 to 80 years. Minors (10–17) travel with a parent or legal guardian who is responsible for them.

Price on request.
The rate depends on the date and group size.

GOOD TO KNOW

No technical difficulty from late spring to autumn. In winter and early spring, snow means crampons and an ice axe are needed: we will advise you on timing and equipment. Summit day is long and requires good fitness.
