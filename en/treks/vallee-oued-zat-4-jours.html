4-DAY TREK

4-day trek in the Oued Zat valley with local families

A trek away from the busy trails, at the pace of the Oued Zat valley villages. You sleep in family homes, share their meals and walk up to the Yagour plateau, known for rock engravings that predate the Common Era.

4 days / 3 nightsDurationMarrakechStartModerateLevelYagour rock engravingsHighlight
Ask about this trek ↗

YOUR ROUTE

Marrakech → Tighdouine → Tlatast → Azgour → Yagour plateau → Aït Inzal → Tighdouine → Marrakech

Day by day

DAY 01 Marrakech – Tighdouine – Tlatast – Azgour village

Leave Marrakech in the morning and arrive in Tighdouine around 10:00. Visit the old village of Tlatast and its pottery workshops, then walk to the village of Azgour near the Oued Zat.

Overnight
With a family in Azgour
Meals
Lunch, dinner
DAY 02 Azgour – Yagour plateau

Walk to the Yagour plateau, famous for its prehistoric rock engravings that predate the Common Era. Time to discover the engravings and the wide open plateau.

Overnight
With a local family or in a camp
Meals
Breakfast, lunch, dinner
DAY 03 Yagour – Aït Inzal area

Cross to the opposite side, into the Aït Inzal area.

Overnight
With a family in the village of Aït Ali (or a nearby village)
Meals
Breakfast, lunch, dinner
DAY 04 Back to Tighdouine – Marrakech

Walk back to Tighdouine, then drive to Marrakech.

Overnight
End of trek (Marrakech not included)
Meals
Breakfast, lunch

HIGHLIGHTS

What you will experience

  • The old village of Tlatast and its pottery workshops
  • The Oued Zat valley and its villages
  • The Yagour plateau rock engravings, older than the Common Era
  • Nights with local families and Berber home cooking

Included

  • Transport Marrakech – Tighdouine – Marrakech
  • Accommodation with local families or in a camp
  • All meals
  • Mules (according to group size)
  • Guide

Not included

  • Accommodation in Marrakech
  • Anything not listed under “Included”

Price on request.
The rate depends on the date and group size.

GOOD TO KNOW

Nights with local families are simple and warm: comfort is basic and rooms are often shared. Stages and host villages may vary with the season and the families’ availability.
